Who we are

Malaysia’s trusted partner for workforce development.

OTC Training Centre Sdn Bhd is an award-winning corporate training provider recognised for practical, relevant and results-driven learning solutions.

Since 2005, we have helped thousands of organisations strengthen workforce capability through public programmes, customised in-house training and learning and development outsourcing. Our certified trainers combine deep subject expertise with real-world experience so participants can apply what they learn.

Today, our reach spans 29 industries and offices in Selangor, Penang and Johor, enabling us to support organisations throughout Peninsular Malaysia, Sabah and Sarawak.
