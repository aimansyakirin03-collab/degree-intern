Before you decide

A little more clarity.

Public or customised: which should I choose?

A public programme follows a published schedule. Customised training lets you discuss content and arrangements around your organisation’s needs.

Can training be delivered online?

OTC offers online options. Confirm the available delivery formats for your selected course or customised arrangement.

How do I check HRD Corp eligibility?

Contact OTC with your chosen programme and organisation details so the team can confirm the applicable process and requirements.

Where can I find fees and current availability?

Open the official course details or contact OTC. Confirm the fee, date, venue and availability before registering.
